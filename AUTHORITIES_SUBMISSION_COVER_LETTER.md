# Authorities / Institutional Submission Cover Letter

To Whom It May Concern,

This document accompanies FreeOSINT.org documentation and educational material for review by institutional, legal, academic, public-interest, or security stakeholders.

FreeOSINT.org is an open educational platform focused on lawful, ethical, privacy-respecting Open Source Intelligence training.

The platform is intended to support:

- learning;
- public-interest research;
- journalism;
- authorized security work;
- responsible analysis of publicly available information;
- digital literacy;
- civic education;
- cybersecurity awareness.

The materials are not intended to authorize:

- surveillance;
- harassment;
- unauthorized access;
- data theft;
- privacy invasion;
- unlawful investigation;
- credential theft;
- doxxing;
- stalking;
- abuse of public information.

Users are instructed to confirm legal authority, respect privacy, minimize data collection, and follow responsible reporting standards.

Repository governance includes:

- responsible-use rules;
- security reporting instructions;
- privacy guidance;
- contribution review;
- content safety review;
- third-party notice requirements;
- legal boundary documentation;
- incident response guidance.

Respectfully,

FreeOSINT.org Maintainers
